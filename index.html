Oluchi,

I’m sorry.

What I did was wrong. You sent me something private, trusting me — and I recorded it without you knowing. That wasn’t a mistake in the moment. It was a choice I made, and I knew better.

Then when you went quiet, I used a piece of it to get your attention. I told myself I just wanted you to reply. That’s not what it was. It was me trying to force a reaction out of you, and I can only imagine what that must have felt like on your side.

I’ve deleted it. The recording, every copy, everywhere. Not because you asked — because it should never have existed in the first place.

You were right to be angry. You were right to say what you said. You were right to walk away. None of that was an overreaction.

I’m not writing this to change your mind. You don’t owe me a reply. You don’t owe me anything at all.

I just didn’t want the last thing between us to be silence. And I didn’t want you spending today wondering whether you were right to be scared. You were.

Today is your day. I hope this year is gentle with you. I hope the people around you are kind to you, and I hope you laugh that laugh that makes your cheeks hurt. I hope the things you’ve been quietly hoping for find their way to you — the big ones and the small ones.

You deserve a day that feels like it was made for you. I hope today is exactly that.

Happy birthday, Oluchi.

— Franklin
